# Experimental Results

This page reports results for BRIDGE rewiring with standard models.

| Dataset | Full BRIDGE | No Optimal P_k |
|---------|-------------|----------------|
| Cora    | 0.85 ± 0.02 | 0.82 ± 0.03    |
| Citeseer| 0.73 ± 0.03 | 0.70 ± 0.04    |

Notes:
- Hard predictions and full resampling are used throughout.
- Selective models and homophily masks are not used.
